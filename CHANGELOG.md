# Changelog

## 26.3.11 (2026-11-24)

- Fluids: mod fluids and their cauldrons are recognised, fluids are drawn with their own texture and name, and flowing
  water reads as Water.
- Item icons keep their components (glint, dye, heads, pot sherds), and containers group stacks by item and
  components.
- Harvest tools come from the game's own tool rules, so mod tools and blocks are right.
- Enchanting power counts mod blocks that add enchanting power.
- Status effects: hidden effects are left out, levels read as in the game, and the list is ordered and scaled as in
  Jade.
- "Leashed by" shows; the From Eye ray-trace option works; the Player List key is your own binding.
- Hints show as toasts.
- More entity details as in Jade: the copper golem's antenna, horse markings, fox and armadillo states, and
  whether a wither is powered.
- A command block's command is shortened past 40 characters; the Friction line is gone.
- Each difference from the original is listed in the README, with its Sunrise issue.
